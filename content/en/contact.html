1. Help and questions

Sign-in problems, questions about the app, joining the beta: write to us. Never send your password or a code received by email.

No confirmation code? Check your spam folder, then tap “Send the code again” in the up-to-date version of the app: at most once a minute and 5 times per 24 hours for the same address. Older codes stop working; a code is valid for 24 hours.

2. Reporting content or behaviour

In the app is fastest: open the profile, conversation or signal, then choose “Report”.

Without an account or the app, anyone can report content they consider illegal (Article 16 of the Digital Services Act). Send to {{mail}}:

  • a precise explanation of what you consider illegal, and why;
  • enough to find the content: first name and age shown, description, date, screenshots if you have any;
  • your name and email address (except for content involving child sexual abuse, which you can report without naming yourself);
  • a sentence confirming that you are making this report in good faith and that your information is accurate.

We acknowledge receipt, examine the report diligently and tell you the outcome and the remedies available.

3. Appealing a decision

Open “Moderation decisions” in the app and choose “Appeal”, or write to us. You have 180 days. See the terms of use.

4. Your personal data

Access, copy, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent: write to {{mail}} from your account's address, with the subject “Personal data”. Reply within one month (may be extended for complex requests). See the privacy policy and account deletion.

Correcting your email address or date of birth

These details cannot be changed in the app. Write to {{mail}} from your account's address; if you no longer have access to it, say so.

Your first name, however, can be changed in the app (My profile, “Edit my profile”). If you cannot do it yourself, write to us in the same way.

  • We check your identity before any correction. In some cases, we may ask to see an identity document, which is neither copied nor kept (procedure to be validated by a lawyer). Do not send one by email.
  • New email address: your sessions are closed and a link is sent to that address, to be confirmed within 48 hours. Until then, your address does not change.
  • A date of birth that would make you a minor is not applied: the account is suspended pending an age check.
