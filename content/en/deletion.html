Delete my ProximaS account

You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time, free of charge, even if your account is suspended or you no longer have the app.

1. How to do it

{{- if on "suppression_dans_app"}}

In the app

Open My profile (“Mon profil”), then Delete my account (“Supprimer mon compte”), and confirm with your password.

{{- else}}

In the app

Deleting your account directly in the app is not available yet. Until then, use one of the options below.

{{- end}} {{- if val "suppression_web_url"}}

On the web, without the app

Enter your account's email address on the request page. You receive a message with a confirmation link, valid for 1 hour: nothing is deleted until you confirm.

Request deletion online

{{- end}}

By email

Write to {{mail}} from your account's email address, with the subject “Account deletion”. We reply to that address to confirm it is really you. Do not send a password or an identity document. If you no longer have access to that address, tell us: we will look for another reasonable way to check your request.

Write to delete my account

2. What happens next

{{- if on "lot5_serveur_deploye"}}
  • As soon as the request is confirmed, your profile is no longer visible and your sessions are closed.
  • You have 14 days to change your mind: signing back in to the app cancels the deletion.
  • After that period, erasure is permanent. An email confirms it.
  • Backups that still contain your data disappear after 35 days at the latest.
{{- else}}
  • We check that the request really comes from you, then erase the account, no later than one month after receiving your request.
  • Erasure is permanent. You receive a reply once it is done.
  • Backups that still contain your data are kept for 35 days, then erased at the next backup.
{{- end}}

3. What is erased

  • the account, email address and password;
  • the profile, photos, answers to questions and current moods;
    • {{- if on "photos_privees"}}
  • your private photos and albums; what you sent or shared can no longer be opened, from the deletion request on (a copy of photos already shown is kept for a while, see below);
    • {{- end}}
  • location, filters and consents;
  • signals sent and received;
  • profile visits, both those you made and those you received;
  • entire conversations, including both people's messages, and meeting suggestions;
  • blocks, sessions and devices registered for notifications.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

4. What is kept, and why

To protect other people and to be able to respond in case of a dispute, some data remains for a limited time (proposed periods, to be validated by a lawyer):

Reports made or received, with their content, and decisions
Your account identifiers are erased; the file is kept for 365 days after the decision. A report not yet decided is kept until it is.
Log of moderation actions
730 days, with an internal account identifier.
{{- if on "photos_privees"}}
Private photos already shown to someone
A copy is kept for 30 days after they disappear, including through erasure of the account; moderation can only view it through a report. A photo attached to a report is kept until the decision, and during an appeal. Photos never shown are erased.
{{- end}} {{- if on "lot7_serveur_deploye"}}
Images reported as involving a minor
Kept under lock, even after the account is deleted, until the retention is lifted (to be validated by a lawyer).
{{- end}}

{{if on "lot5_serveur_deploye"}}In these two cases only, a fingerprint of the address, not the address itself, is kept for 365 days after erasure, to refuse a new sign-up with it. Nothing is kept for this purpose if the account had been reinstated. 5. Get your data before you leave

{{- if on "export_dans_app"}}

In the app, open My profile (“Mon profil”), then Download my data (“Télécharger mes données”). You get an archive with your data, including profile visits (your visitors as the app shows them to you), and your photos{{if on "photos_privees"}} (for private ones: yours in your gallery or still visible, never those you received){{end}}; messages and texts written by other people are not included. Do this before deleting your account.

{{- else}}

Ask for a copy of your data at {{mail}} before requesting deletion: downloading it from the app is not available yet.

{{- end}}

6. Erasing only part of it

Without deleting your account, you can also remove photos, erase profile answers or your moods, turn notifications off or withdraw a consent (withdrawing the location one erases your location). “Visit discreetly” stops new visits from being recorded; earlier ones are erased 30 days after the latest visit. For anything else, write to us. See your rights.
