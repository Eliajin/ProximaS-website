My sky: explore instead of scrolling

Your sky of stars

Compatible people appear as stars: 50 at most, grouped in constellations of 10, from the nearest to the farthest. Where a star sits on screen is decorative: it shows neither a direction nor an exact distance.

Compatible both ways

You only see people whose gender matches what you are looking for, and who are also looking for people of your gender. The age ranges each person chooses apply both ways.

Your filters, and a grid

Age, with a photo, same mood, recently active, and “Limit the distance” (optional, from 1 to 100 km), which only affects what you see. Prefer a grid? It shows the same people, constellation by constellation, with their photo if they have one, their first name and their age. Distance and moods are shown on their profile.

What you feel like doing

A walk, a drink, a concert, a film, a meal, sport, a game or just a chat: say what you would like to do tonight, tomorrow or this weekend. You can pick up to 3 for the same time.

When someone shares at least one of your wishes for a time that overlaps yours, their star gets a blue ring. Times follow your local time, and your wishes disappear by themselves once the time has passed. They are optional.

A signal, then a conversation

Start from a detail

A signal replies to one precise item on a profile: a question, a photo or a mood. 240 characters at most, 5 signals a day (reset at midnight, your local time).

Reply or pass

A conversation only opens if the other person replies. Passing does not notify anyone. A signal with no reply expires after 14 days and you must wait 30 days after sending it before sending another one to the same person. After a passed signal, this is possible after 24 hours: the sender can then guess that the earlier one had been passed.

Chat, then meet

Text messaging{{if on "photos_privees"}}, private photos and albums (not checked beforehand; screenshots blocked on Android only){{end}} and a way to suggest a time to meet (activity, date, hour), without sharing a location. Notifications are optional and only say “You have something new”.

Your privacy

  • Your location is rounded to a grid of about 500 m (up to about 1 km from east to west in northern Europe) before it is saved, and only the latest one is kept. The app asks for it while you use it, not in the background.
  • Others never receive your location: only an approximate distance band, never an exact distance.
  • While the publisher keeps the “no limit” option open, compatible people who do not limit the distance can see your profile even if they are far away, whatever your own limit. The publisher may restore a maximum limit of 100 km later.
  • You can hide your distance: it then appears neither in the sky, nor on your profile, nor in your conversations. You can still appear, in a randomly chosen spot, to compatible people: if they limit the distance, only when you are less than 25 km from them; otherwise, whatever the distance.
  • You can switch to invisible mode: your profile disappears from other people's sky and grid, and your conversations remain, with no distance. People you have a conversation with, or you sent a signal that is still pending and has not expired, can still open your profile and see your profile photos.
  • Your sexual orientation appears on your profile page only if you turn on “Show my orientation”; otherwise it is hidden and is not stored.
  • Location data embedded in your photos (EXIF) is removed.
  • When you open someone's profile, they can see it under “Viewed your profile”: only the latest visit is kept, for 30 days, with no notification. With “Visit discreetly” or invisible mode, your visits are not recorded, and you do not see who viewed your profile.
  • The app shows no advertising today and contains no advertising or analytics tool. If advertising were added later, the privacy policy would be updated first.
