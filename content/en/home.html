My sky: explore instead of scrolling

Stars around you

Compatible people near you appear as stars: 50 at most, grouped in constellations of 10, from the nearest to the farthest. Where a star sits on screen is decorative: it shows neither a direction nor an exact distance.

Compatible both ways

You only see people whose gender matches what you are looking for, and who are also looking for people of your gender. The age ranges each person chooses apply both ways.

Your filters, and a list

Age, distance, with a photo, same mood, recently active. Prefer a list? It shows the same information.

What you feel like doing

A walk, a drink, a concert, a film, a meal, sport, a game or just a chat: say what you would like to do tonight, tomorrow or this weekend.

When someone shares the same wish for a time that overlaps yours, their star gets a blue ring. Your wish disappears by itself once the time has passed. It is optional.

A signal, then a conversation

Start from a detail

A signal replies to one precise item on a profile: a question, a photo or a mood. 240 characters at most, 5 signals a day (reset at midnight, Brussels time).

Reply or pass

A conversation only opens if the other person replies. Passing stays discreet: nobody is told. A signal with no reply expires after 14 days.

Chat, then meet

Text messaging and a way to suggest a time to meet (activity, date, hour), without sharing a location. Notifications are optional and only say that there is something new.

People's safety

  • You can block someone at any time: you no longer see each other, and they are not told.
  • You can report a profile, a photo, a signal or a conversation.
  • Photos are checked before they become visible, and reports are reviewed by a person. Our target is to handle them within 24 hours.
  • Moderation decisions come with reasons and can be challenged.
  • The service is forbidden to anyone under 18. During the beta, age is calculated from the date of birth you declare: no external check is in place yet.

No app can guarantee who is on the other side. Stay careful, especially before a first date. Read our safety advice.

Your privacy

  • Your location is rounded to a grid of about 500 m before it is saved, and only the latest one is kept. The app asks for it while you use it, not in the background.
  • Others never receive your location: only an approximate distance.
  • You can hide your distance: it then appears neither in the sky, nor on your profile, nor in your conversations. Compatible people less than 25 km away can still see you, in a randomly chosen spot.
  • You can switch to invisible mode: your profile disappears from other people's sky, and your conversations remain.
  • Your sexual orientation is displayed, and stored, only if you choose to show it.
  • Location data embedded in your photos (EXIF) is removed.
  • The app shows no advertising today and contains no advertising or analytics tool. If advertising were added later, the privacy policy would be updated first.
