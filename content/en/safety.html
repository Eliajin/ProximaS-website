Your safety

What ProximaS does, what it cannot do, and the habits worth keeping.

If you are in danger

Call 112, the European emergency number, or your country's emergency number immediately. In Belgium, the police can also be reached on 101. ProximaS does not replace emergency services.

Before a first date

  • Take your time: do not give out your number, address or workplace too quickly.
  • Choose a busy public place.
  • Tell someone close to you where you are going, with whom, and when you expect to be home.
  • Get there and leave by your own means.
  • Keep an eye on your drink and keep your phone charged.
  • You can leave at any time, without explaining. You owe nobody anything.

Money, pictures and blackmail

  • Never send money, prepaid cards, cryptocurrency or a code received by text message or email, whatever the story.
  • Be wary of someone who wants to leave the app quickly, keeps postponing meeting or talks about investments.
  • Do not send intimate photos: they can be used to blackmail you. Sexually explicit content is in any case forbidden on ProximaS, even on request.
  • If you are being blackmailed, do not pay, keep the evidence (screenshots), block, report, and contact the police.

Private photos and albums

  • Nobody checks them before they are sent: you may receive an unwanted image. It arrives blurred and is only displayed if you touch it. Sexually explicit content is forbidden: report it, it can be removed and its sender sanctioned.
  • “Refuse photos” blocks new photos and hides albums already opened to you.
  • Screenshots are blocked on Android only, not on iPhone, and another device can photograph the screen. “View once” or “24 hours” therefore do not prevent someone from keeping a trace.
  • You can withdraw a photo or access to an album, but not erase what has already been seen or copied.
  • Never pass on someone's photo without their consent. To report a photo or album you received, even if it recently disappeared, do it from the app, without making a copy.

Blocking and reporting

  • Block, from a profile, a conversation or a signal received: you no longer see each other and can no longer exchange. The person is not told.
  • Passing a signal is not blocking: the same person can send you a new one from 24 hours later. If they insist, block them, and report them if it is harassment.
  • Report a profile, a photo, a conversation or a signal, with a reason: seems to be under 18, harassment or threats, unwanted sexual content,{{if on "lot7_serveur_deploye"}} content involving a minor,{{end}} scam or request for money, fake profile or impersonation, hateful or discriminatory remarks, other. The reported person is not told who reported them.
  • For a conversation, the last 20 messages are attached only if you tick the box. Remember to do so if they show the problem.
  • Reports are reviewed by a person. We aim to handle them within 24 hours, but cannot guarantee it: if you are in danger, do not wait for our reply.
  • Without the app, write to {{mail}} (see how to report).

Profile visits

When you open a person's profile, they can see it under “Viewed your profile” for 30 days. If you do not want this, turn on “Visit discreetly” (in My profile, up-to-date version of the app) or invisible mode before opening the profile. You will then not see who viewed yours. How visits work.

18 and over only

ProximaS is forbidden to minors. During the beta, age is calculated from the declared date of birth: no external check is in place yet, and someone can lie. If someone seems to be a minor, stop the conversation and report them: these reports take priority and can lead to immediate suspension pending a check.{{if on "masquage_photos_age"}} When more than 5 different accounts have such a report awaiting a decision, the account's photos are temporarily hidden from other people, until a moderator makes a decision.{{end}} No sexual content involving a minor is tolerated: see child protection.

Protecting your location

Others do not receive your location: at most an approximate distance band, never an exact one. People far away can see your profile if they do not limit the distance; your own limit does not change this. You can hide this distance, switch to invisible mode or withdraw your “location” consent (your location is then erased and your profile disabled). In invisible mode, people you have a conversation with, or you sent a signal that is still pending and has not expired, can still open your profile, with no distance: to close this access to someone, block them. No setting offers absolute protection. A determined person may try to guess your area: do not give details about the places you usually go. How location and distance work.
