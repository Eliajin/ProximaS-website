Your safety

What ProximaS does, what it cannot do, and the habits worth keeping.

If you are in danger

Call 112, the European emergency number, or your country's emergency number immediately. In Belgium, the police can also be reached on 101. ProximaS does not replace emergency services.

Before a first date

  • Take your time: do not give out your number, address or workplace too quickly.
  • Choose a busy public place.
  • Tell someone close to you where you are going, with whom, and when you expect to be home.
  • Get there and leave by your own means.
  • Keep an eye on your drink and keep your phone charged.
  • You can leave at any time, without explaining. You owe nobody anything.

Money, pictures and blackmail

  • Never send money, prepaid cards, cryptocurrency or a code received by text message or email, whatever the story.
  • Be wary of someone who wants to leave the app quickly, keeps postponing meeting or talks about investments.
  • Do not send intimate photos: they can be used to blackmail you.
  • If you are being blackmailed, do not pay, keep the evidence (screenshots), block, report, and contact the police.

Blocking and reporting

  • Block, from a profile, a conversation or a signal received: you no longer see each other and can no longer exchange. The person is not told.
  • Report a profile, a photo, a conversation or a signal, with a reason: possible minor, harassment or threats, unwanted sexual content, scam or request for money, fake profile, hate speech, other. The reported person is not told who reported them.
  • For a conversation, the last 20 messages are attached only if you tick the box. Remember to do so if they show the problem.
  • Reports are reviewed by a person. We aim to handle them within 24 hours, but cannot guarantee it: if you are in danger, do not wait for our reply.
  • Without the app, write to {{mail}} (see how to report).

18 and over only

ProximaS is forbidden to minors. During the beta, age is calculated from the declared date of birth: no external check is in place yet, and someone can lie. If someone seems to be a minor, stop the conversation and report them: these reports take priority and can lead to immediate suspension pending a check.

What we do, and our limits

  • Photos are checked before they become visible; profile texts may be flagged for review.
  • {{if on "relecture_messages_prives"}}Messages may be automatically flagged for review, but they are not all read.{{else}}Private messages are not automatically reviewed: without a report, moderation does not know what happens in them.{{end}}
  • We check neither people's identity nor their background.
  • An approved photo does not prove that the person is who they claim to be.

Protecting your location

Others only see an approximate distance. You can hide it, switch to invisible mode or withdraw access to your location. A determined person may still try to guess your area: do not give details about the places you usually go. How location and distance work.
