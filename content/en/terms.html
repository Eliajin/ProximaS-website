7. How your sky is put together

The selection and order of profiles are calculated automatically, using these criteria:

  • compatibility both ways: each person's gender matches what the other is looking for;
  • age both ways: each person falls within the age range chosen by the other;
  • your filters: maximum distance (25 km by default), with a photo, same mood (at least one wish in common, for overlapping times), active in the last 7 days;
  • proximity: the 50 nearest profiles at most, grouped in 5 constellations of 10; within a constellation, the order is shuffled every day;
  • profiles that hide their distance are searched within a 25 km radius and placed at random;
  • blocked, invisible or suspended profiles, and those without a recent location, are not shown.

Nobody can pay to be ranked higher, and neither your messages nor your visits are used to rank profiles.
