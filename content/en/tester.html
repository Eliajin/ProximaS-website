Become a tester

ProximaS is in beta. You can sign up for the test yourself; it is for people aged 18 and over only.

Before you start

  • You must be at least 18. No minors are admitted, either to the test or to the app.
  • The app is being tested: it may contain errors, change or be interrupted.
  • Read the terms of use, the privacy policy and the safety advice.

On Android: two steps, in this order

{{- if and (val "lien_groupe_google") (val "lien_test_play")}}

Use the same Google account for both steps: the one you are signed in with on Google Play on your phone.

  1. Step 1: join the Google group

    Sign in with that account, open the testers' group and join it. Without this step, the next one does not work.

    Join the Google group

  2. Step 2: become a tester

    With the same Google account, open the test page on Google Play and tap “Become a tester”.

    Become a tester

What happens next?

  • The test page then offers to download ProximaS on Google Play. Install it on an Android phone signed in with that same account.
  • This may not be immediate. If the test page or Google Play does not offer the app yet, check the account you used and step 1, then try again a little later.
  • Then open the app and create your ProximaS account, which is separate from your Google account.

What going through Google or Apple involves

  • This website has no form and collects nothing for the test: the links lead to Google or Apple, whose rules apply.
    • {{- if val "lien_groupe_google"}}
  • The publisher administers the Google group: they see the email address and display name of your account, and other members might see them depending on the group's settings. See the test group in the privacy policy.
    • {{- end}}
  • You can stop at any time by leaving the test. Uninstalling the app does not delete your ProximaS account: see account deletion.
